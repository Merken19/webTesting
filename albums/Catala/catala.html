Català

Go to erenerken.art

1 - L'avi Siset em parlava

Pa’ Siset was speaking to me

2 - De bon matí al portal,

One morning at the doorway,

3 - Mentres al Sol esperàvem

While we wait under the sun

4 - I els carros vèiem passar.

As the carts went and passed.

5 - Siset que no veus l'estaca

Siset, can’t you see that stake

6 - A on estem tots lligats?

To which we're all tied?

7 - Si no podem desfe'ns-en,

Unless we can loosen,

8 - Mai no podrem caminar!

We will never walk!

9 - Si estirem tots, ella caurà

But if we pull you’ll see it fall

10 - I molt de temps no pot durar,

It cannot last much more at all,

11 - Segur que tomba, tomba, tomba

You’ll see it tumble, tumble, tumble

12 - Ben corcada deu ser ja.

Its core got to be rotten well.

13 - Si jo l'estiro fort per aqui

If I grab my part and pull for you

14 - I tu l'estires fort per allà,

And you grab yours and pull for me,

15 - Segur que tomba, tomba, tomba

You’ll see it tumble, tumble, tumble

16 - I ens podrem alliberar.

Only then we will be free.
